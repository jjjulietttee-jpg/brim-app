How to Remember to Take Creatine Every Day

Updated October 2026 · By the Brim developer

Short answer. Tie the dose to something you already do each day, set one reminder at that time, and log it with a single tap so the habit has a visible result. Brim sends reminders (adaptive timing with Pro) and shows your saturation percentage rising when you dose, so a skipped day is visible rather than silent.

Step by step

  1. Pick an anchorChoose a daily moment such as breakfast or your post-workout shake and take creatine then.
  2. Turn on remindersAllow notifications in Brim and set your time. Pro adds adaptive reminders.
  3. Log in one tapTap the dose on the Today screen, or from the reminder, so the day counts.
  4. Add the widget if you likeWith Pro, a Home Screen widget shows the ring and day state and lets you log without opening the app.

Frequently asked questions

What if I miss a day of creatine?

One skipped day costs little. Brim's model drops saturation by 5% of the gap above the baseline per skipped day, so a single miss is small and you recover quickly the next day. Several skipped weeks bring it back toward baseline.

Should I double up after a missed day?

No. Take your normal dose; doubling adds nothing to saturation and can upset your stomach.

Does the time of day matter?

Not for saturation: what matters is taking it consistently. Pick the time you will actually remember.

Does Brim have a widget?

Yes, a Home Screen widget in Brim Pro.

Do I need to log in?

No account is needed.

Is logging free?

Yes. Logging a dose is free; Pro adds forecast, history, adaptive reminders and the widget.
