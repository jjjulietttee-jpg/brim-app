Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 August 2026

This Policy explains what Brim ("Brim", "we", "us") does with information when you use the Brim app on iPhone or Android. The short version: Brim has no accounts and no servers of its own. Your dose history, your answers and your saturation estimate live on your phone and nowhere else.

1. What Brim stores on your device

  • Your dose log — the date and the number of grams you record, one row per day.
  • Your plan — the answers you gave during setup (body weight, goal, preferred timing, whether you were already supplementing) and the protocol derived from them.
  • Your saturation estimate — calculated on your device from the above.
  • Reminder settings — notifications are scheduled locally by your phone's operating system. We do not operate a push server.

All of this is written to a private database inside Brim's own app storage. It is not uploaded anywhere, and there is no way for us to read it.

2. Health data — Apple Health and Health Connect

Brim only ever reads health data, and never writes it. If you grant permission, Brim reads two things: your body weight, to keep your recommended dose accurate as your weight changes, and your workouts or exercise sessions, to mark training days in the app.

Health data is used on your device for those two purposes and nothing else. It is never transmitted off your device, never used for advertising or marketing, never sold, and never disclosed to any third party — including our analytics provider. We do not share it with any other app, and we do not retain a copy of it beyond what the app needs while it is running.

Health access is optional; Brim works fully without it. You can grant or revoke it at any time:

  • iPhone — Settings → Privacy & Security → Health → Brim. Apple does not tell apps whether read permission was granted, which is why Brim never claims a permission was approved.
  • Android — Settings → Security & privacy → Health Connect → App permissions → Brim, or from Health Connect directly. Revoking permission stops any further reading at once.

Note that creatine is not a data type Apple Health or Health Connect supports, so your doses cannot be written there even if you wanted them to be.

3. Purchases

Subscriptions are sold and processed by Apple through the App Store on iPhone, and by Google through Google Play on Android. Brim is told only whether you currently have an active subscription. We never see or receive your name, card details or billing address.

4. Diagnostics and usage analytics

Brim uses Google Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics to count anonymous, aggregated events (for example: a screen was opened, a purchase completed, the app crashed) together with standard technical data — device model, operating system version, app version, and a random installation identifier generated by the SDK.

This data is not linked to your identity, and no content from your dose log, your plan or your health data is ever included in it.

5. What Brim does not do

  • No account, sign-in, name, email address or phone number.
  • No camera, photo library, microphone, contacts or location access.
  • No servers of our own — the app makes no network requests other than those made by the platform's and Firebase's own SDKs.
  • No tracking across apps or websites. On iPhone, Brim does not use the App Tracking Transparency framework and does not access the advertising identifier (IDFA). On Android, Brim does not request the AD_ID permission and advertising-ID collection is switched off in the Firebase SDK.
  • No advertising, and no selling or sharing of personal information.

6. Third parties

  • Apple — App Store purchases, subscription management, and (with your permission) Apple Health on your device.
  • Google Play — purchases and subscription management on Android.
  • Health Connect — an Android system component; with your permission it passes weight and exercise data to Brim on your device.
  • Google Firebase — anonymous analytics and crash reporting.

Each processes data under its own terms. No other third party receives anything.

7. Retention and deletion

Because your data is stored only on your device, deleting Brim deletes all of it — dose history, plan and settings — immediately and permanently. There is no server copy for us to delete and no export for us to hand over. Revoking health access, in iOS Settings or in Health Connect, stops any further reading at once.

To ask about diagnostic data associated with your installation identifier, email us at the address below.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or port your personal data, or to object to its processing (for example under the GDPR or the CCPA). Since Brim holds your data only on your own device, you can exercise all of these directly and immediately from the app or by deleting it. For anything else, write to us.

9. Children

Brim is not directed to children and we do not knowingly collect anything from them. It gives guidance about a dietary supplement, which is not appropriate for minors without medical supervision.

10. Not medical advice

Brim is an informational and tracking tool, not a medical device. The saturation percentage is a model estimate calculated from the doses you log using published creatine kinetics — it is not a measurement of your body and not a test result. Nothing in Brim diagnoses, treats, cures or prevents any condition. Talk to a qualified healthcare professional before starting any supplement, particularly if you are pregnant or breastfeeding, have kidney or liver conditions, or take prescription medication.

11. Security

Your records stay in Brim's private app storage, protected by operating-system app sandboxing and device encryption. Traffic from the platform and Firebase SDKs is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS). No system is perfectly secure, but keeping your data off the internet entirely removes most of the risk.
