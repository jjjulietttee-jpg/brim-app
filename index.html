Frequently asked questions

How does Brim work out my dose?

From your body weight. The maintenance dose follows the ISSN range of 3-5 g per day (about 0.03 g/kg), banded so that heavier people get more, and the loading dose is about 0.3 g/kg per day, rounded to the nearest 5 g and capped at 30 g.

Is Brim free?

Logging a dose is free. Brim Pro unlocks the saturation forecast, full history and calendar, adaptive reminders, the full article library and the Home Screen widget: $4.99 per month or $29.99 per year, each with a 3-day free trial.

Is the saturation number a medical measurement?

No. It is an estimate from a published kinetics model fitted to the ISSN 2017 position stand, not a blood or muscle test. Brim says so in the app and is not medical advice.

Is Brim medical advice?

No. It is an informational estimate based on published research, not a diagnosis or a substitute for a doctor.

Does Brim use AI?

No.

Does it track water or other supplements?

No, creatine only.
