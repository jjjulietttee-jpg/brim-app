How Long Does Creatine Loading Take?

Updated October 2026 · By the Brim developer

Short answer. Loading at about 0.3 g/kg per day (roughly 20-25 g for most adults, split into servings) saturates muscle in about 5-7 days. Taking 3-5 g a day gets you to the same place in about 28 days. Brim shows both on a saturation curve and gives you the date for your own weight.

Step by step

  1. Choose loading or standardLoading is faster; standard is gentler on the stomach. Both end at the same maintenance dose.
  2. Split the loading doseBrim suggests splitting a loading dose into several servings rather than taking it at once.
  3. Log every dayMissing days slows saturation, so log each dose.
  4. Switch to maintenanceWhen the ring reaches full, drop to your maintenance dose (protocol switching is in Pro).

Frequently asked questions

How long does it take to saturate on 3-5 g a day?

About 28 days according to the ISSN 2017 position stand, which Brim's model is fitted to.

How long does loading take?

About 5-7 days at roughly 0.3 g/kg per day.

Do I need to load?

No. Both routes reach the same saturation; loading just gets there sooner.

What if I skip days during loading?

Saturation rises more slowly and Brim's target date moves later.

How long until it wears off after I stop?

The ISSN figure is 4-6 weeks to return to baseline, which Brim's model reproduces.

Is there an app that tracks loading?

Brim supports both protocols and shows the days to full saturation; some other creatine trackers log doses without a loading model.
